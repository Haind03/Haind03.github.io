I'm HaiND, a security student at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT) in Vietnam. Most of my time goes into reverse engineering, malware analysis and digital forensics, plus a fair amount of vulnerability research and threat hunting.

I play CTFs with the PTIT team, usually on the reversing and forensics side, in both Jeopardy and Attack-Defense formats. Over the last couple of years that has taken us from the school's own contest to the ASEAN finals, and in December 2025 to Russia, where we won M\*CTF. I've also been on the other side of the table, writing reverse engineering and forensics challenges for the PTIT Information Security contest in 2024 and 2025 and handling their deployment for the qualifying and final rounds.

This blog is where I put my notes. The biggest part is the [Technique Reverse](/technique-reverse/) series, which I wrote while learning RE, along with a few malware analysis write-ups from samples and labs I've worked on.

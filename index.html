whoami

I like taking things apart to see how they really work. In security that usually means a binary nobody gave me the source for, a piece of malware that doesn't want to be read, or a disk image that still remembers what happened on it.

I'm a Cybersecurity Teaching Assistant at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT), where I help run security courses, support academic research and train students for competitions. Before that I spent a little over three years at BlueCyber, first as an intern doing reverse engineering and malware analysis, then full time on threat hunting, tracking down persistent threats and working incident response investigations.

Outside of work I look for bugs in WordPress plugins and report them through Wordfence, and I play a lot of CTF, usually the reversing and forensics side. With the PTIT team that has taken me from the school contest to the ASEAN finals and to an Attack-Defense final in Russia, which we won.

Hi, I'm Hai, though most people online know me as HaiND. I like taking things apart to see how they really work, and in security that usually means a binary nobody gave me the source for, a piece of malware that doesn't want to be read, or a disk image that still remembers what happened on it.

I'm currently a Teaching Assistant in cybersecurity at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT), where I help run classes, support academic research and train students for competitions. Before that I spent a little over three years at BlueCyber, first as an intern doing reverse engineering and malware analysis, then full time on threat hunting, tracking down persistent threats and working incident response investigations.

Outside of work I hunt for bugs in web software, mostly WordPress plugins, and report them through Wordfence. And I play a lot of CTF. I usually take the reversing and forensics challenges, and with the PTIT team I've played everything from the school contest to the ASEAN finals and an Attack-Defense final in Russia.

This blog is my notebook. The Technique Reverse series is everything I wish someone had handed me when I started learning RE, and the malware analysis posts are write-ups of samples and labs I've worked through.

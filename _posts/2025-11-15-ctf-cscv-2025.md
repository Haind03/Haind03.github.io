CSCV 2025 was the last contest of our university years, and I am glad it ended the way it did. CSCV, the Cybersecurity Student Contest Vietnam, is the national student security contest run by the National Cybersecurity Association, with the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Education and Training as patrons. I played Bracket B, the Jeopardy one, with team PTIT.0verflow, and we took second prize.

![The PTIT.0verflow team in front of the CSCV 2025 contest page](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/team.webp)
_Team PTIT.0verflow._

Some contests you walk into just wanting to solve as much as you can and finish high. This one was different. By the time it came around it was not only a contest, it was the last stop in a run that started years earlier, when I barely knew what CTF was.

## How we got here

I came into this as a complete beginner, staring at challenges with no idea where to start, half convinced I was not cut out for it. From [that first consolation prize at PTIT CTF 2022](/posts/ctf-ptit-ctf-2022/) it grew into a few years of contests at home and abroad, from [Attack-Defense finals](/posts/ctf-asean-2024-attack-defense/) to [Korea](/posts/ctf-hacktheon-sejong-2025/) and [Da Nang](/posts/ctf-digital-dragons-2025/), with weekends spent practicing, a few wins, a few near-misses, and plenty of challenges we never cracked. Somewhere in there CTF stopped being an extracurricular and became the thing I actually studied, and the people I practiced with became the reason I kept showing up.

## The contest

In the qualifier we finished 10th with 1,815 points, just enough to make the final.

![The CSCV 2025 qualifying round top 10](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/qualifier-top10.webp)
_Qualifying round top 10._

The final had 20 teams in Bracket B. It went the way these usually do, read a challenge, decide whether to keep digging or move on, split the work, hold the pace. When the scoreboard froze we were 6th, and from then on we were solving blind, no idea whether other teams were quietly pulling flags we could not see.

When the results came out on November 15 we had moved up to 3rd, which meant second prize in Bracket B. Going from 6th at the freeze to 3rd after it was the best moment of the day.

![The CSCV 2025 Bracket B final results, with PTIT.0verflow highlighted with a second prize](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/final-results.webp)
_Bracket B results, November 15, 2025. PTIT.0verflow is circled._

## A good place to stop

I was happy with the second prize, but that is not really why this one stuck with me. It closed a chapter. The university years gave us the chance to meet, practice together and play in rooms we never thought we would sit in, and this was the last time we did it as students.

What I will remember about CTF was never only the flags or the rankings. It was sitting around a problem together, arguing over one small detail, the groan when an approach failed and the noise when it finally worked, the weekend practice, the trips. After we leave school everyone drifts toward their own path and the free weekends will not come as easily. But the way of thinking, the habit of staying calm in front of a problem with no given answer, and the friendships are not going anywhere.

So, thanks to my teammates for all of it, and to CTF for making these years something I can look back on and smile about. We ended on a good note, and I could not have asked for a better last one.

CSCV 2025 (Cybersecurity Student Contest Vietnam) is the national student security contest run by the National Cybersecurity Association (NCA), with the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Education and Training as patrons. I played Bracket B, the Jeopardy one, with team PTIT.0verflow. We got second prize.

![The PTIT.0verflow team in front of the CSCV 2025 contest page](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/team.webp)
_The four of us from PTIT.0verflow, with the CSCV 2025 page on the projector._

The funny part is how we got into the final. The top 10 teams from the qualifying round went through, and we were 10th. Last spot.

![The CSCV 2025 qualifying round top 10, with five teams tied on 1,815 points](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/qualifier-top10.webp)
_Qualifying round top 10. Places 6 to 10 all have 1,815 points._

Places 6 to 10 all had exactly 1,815 points: EHCenter, PTIT.CBS (also from our school), HCMUT, Breaking Bad and us. Five teams on the same score, and we were the last one through on the tiebreak. One flag less and we would have been out.

Since we barely made it, there wasn't much pressure going into the final. We just played. And it went well: when the Bracket B results came out on November 15, we had second prize. UIT-Reze won first, KMA.0xC4ng and us got second, and Breaking Bad, one of the teams we were tied with in the qualifier, got third. There was also a team from Institut Teknologi Bandung in Indonesia, and 16 teams in total got prizes.

![The CSCV 2025 Bracket B final results, with PTIT.0verflow highlighted with a second prize](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/final-results.webp)
_Bracket B results, November 15, 2025. PTIT.0verflow is circled._

10th in the qualifier, second prize in the final. Pretty happy with how this one turned out.

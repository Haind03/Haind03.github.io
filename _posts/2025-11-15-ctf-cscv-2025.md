CSCV 2025 (Cybersecurity Student Contest Vietnam) is the national student security contest run by the National Cybersecurity Association (NCA), with the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Education and Training as patrons. That year each school could send one team per bracket. PTIT had PTIT.CBS in one bracket, and I played Bracket B, the Jeopardy one, with team PTIT.0verflow. We got second prize.

![The PTIT.0verflow team in front of the CSCV 2025 contest page](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/team.webp)
_Team PTIT.0verflow._

In the qualifying round we were in the top 10, in 10th place with 1,815 points. Places 6 to 10 all ended on the same 1,815.

![The CSCV 2025 qualifying round top 10](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/qualifier-top10.webp)
_Qualifying round top 10._

The Bracket B results came out on November 15. UIT-Reze got first prize, KMA.0xC4ng and PTIT.0verflow got second, and Breaking Bad, KMA.UniverSea and L3_u5th got third. Bracket B had 20 teams in the final, including one from Institut Teknologi Bandung in Indonesia, and there were 16 prizes in total.

![The CSCV 2025 Bracket B final results, with PTIT.0verflow highlighted with a second prize](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/final-results.webp)
_Bracket B results, November 15, 2025. PTIT.0verflow is circled._

Pretty happy with how this one turned out.

The Cybersecurity Student Contest Vietnam 2025, CSCV for short, is the national student cybersecurity competition run by the National Cybersecurity Association (NCA) under the patronage of the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Education and Training. I played it in Bracket B, the Jeopardy bracket, with team PTIT.0verflow. On November 15, 2025 the final results came out and we had second prize.

What makes this one special to me isn't where we finished. It's how close we came to not being in the final at all.

![The PTIT.0verflow team in front of the CSCV 2025 contest page](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/team.webp)
_PTIT.0verflow, the four of us, in front of the CSCV 2025 contest page at PTIT._

## Tenth out of ten

The qualifying round sent the top 10 teams through to the final. When the results were published, PTIT.0verflow was on the list in tenth place, the very last spot, with 1,815 points.

![The CSCV 2025 qualifying round top 10, with five teams tied on 1,815 points](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/qualifier-top10.webp)
_The qualifying round top 10. Places six to ten are all on 1,815 points, and PTIT.0verflow is the last of them._

Look at the bottom half of that table. Places six through ten are all on exactly 1,815 points: EHCenter, our fellow PTIT team PTIT.CBS, HCMUT, Breaking Bad and us. Five teams with the same score, separated only by tiebreak, and we were the one at the end of the line. One fewer flag, or the same flag a little later, and we would have watched the final from the outside. It's a strange feeling to qualify and feel lucky and slightly embarrassed at the same time.

## The final

Getting in by the skin of our teeth turned out to be useful. Nobody expected anything from the tenth-placed team, including, honestly, us, so we went into the final with nothing to protect and played it that way. Jeopardy finals reward a team that splits the board well and doesn't get stuck, and that day we didn't get stuck for long.

When the Bracket B results came out on November 15, PTIT.0verflow had a second prize, one of two second prizes behind UIT-Reze, who took first. Breaking Bad, one of the teams we'd been tied with on 1,815 in the qualifier, finished with a third prize. The field included strong teams from across Vietnam and a team from Institut Teknologi Bandung in Indonesia, and sixteen teams in total ended up on the prize list.

![The CSCV 2025 Bracket B final results, with PTIT.0verflow highlighted with a second prize](/assets/img/posts/cscv-2025/final-results.webp)
_Bracket B final results, November 15, 2025. PTIT.0verflow, circled, with second prize._

## What I took from it

Two things. First, the qualifier and the final are different contests, and the line between in and out is often thinner than it looks: five teams on the same score, one of them goes through last. If you're on the right side of that line, use it. Second, playing without pressure is a real advantage, and it's worth trying to bring that feeling into contests where you are the favourite, which is a lot harder.

From last qualifying spot to second prize is my favourite kind of result, the kind that only makes sense if you were there for both halves of it.

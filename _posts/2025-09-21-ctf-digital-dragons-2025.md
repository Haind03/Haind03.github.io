In September 2025 I went to Da Nang for Digital Dragons, The Cybersecurity Challenge 2025, with team BlueCyber_Intern. The contest was on September 20 and the closing ceremony was on the 21st at VKU. We got a consolation prize.

Big thanks to BlueCyber for sponsoring us. The company covered the trip so we could just go there and play. I learned a lot of what I know about malware and threat hunting at BlueCyber, first as an intern and then full time, so playing under the company's name felt right.

Digital Dragons is a Jeopardy CTF organized by evvolabs, VKU and the National Cybersecurity Association (NCA). The challenges were meant to look like real-world exploitation, and the official name is literally a "talent search" contest, so the level was decent.

![The closing ceremony of Digital Dragons 2025 at VKU, Da Nang](/assets/img/posts/digital-dragons-2025/closing-ceremony.webp)
_Closing ceremony at VKU, September 21, 2025._

The contest went the way Jeopardy usually goes. The first part is fast, everyone clears the easy ones in their own category. After that it gets slower and harder, and you have to decide whether to keep going on something you've been stuck on for a while or switch to help someone else. We made a few wrong calls there, like most teams do.

Near the end we were somewhere around the edge of the prize places, so we kept refreshing the scoreboard and kept pushing until time ran out. We weren't sure if we'd made it.

We did. Getting the consolation prize at the ceremony the next day was mostly relief, then happiness.

![The consolation prize board from Digital Dragons 2025](/assets/img/posts/digital-dragons-2025/consolation-prize.webp)
_Consolation prize, Digital Dragons 2025._

Not the biggest prize, but a nice weekend, and a reminder that I still need to get better at knowing when to drop a challenge. Thanks again to BlueCyber.

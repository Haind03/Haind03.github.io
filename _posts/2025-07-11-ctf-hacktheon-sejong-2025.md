In July 2025 my team PTIT.BlueCyber flew to Sejong, South Korea for the HackTheon Sejong 2025 final. It is an international student security competition hosted by the city of Sejong, backed by NIS, the Ministry of Science and ICT and KISA, with a 38 million won prize pool. It was my first time going abroad for a CTF, and we finished 10th.

First, thanks to PTIT. The school paid for the whole trip and sent us, and we could not have gone otherwise.

![Our table, number 33, PTIT.BlueCyber, before the final started](/assets/img/posts/hacktheon-sejong-2025/our-table.webp)
_Table 33, just before the start._

The organizers looked after us well. A nice hotel, everything arranged, so we could just rest and focus on the contest. For a first trip out of the country to play CTF, being hosted that carefully was already more than I expected.

The final ran on July 10, from 10:00 to 18:00. The morning went well and at one point we were 4th. Then the afternoon turned into other teams steadily solving while we slid down the board, one place at a time. Our last flag went in at 17:31:40, about half an hour before the end, and put us on 3,764 points.

![The final scoreboard of HackTheon Sejong 2025](/assets/img/posts/hacktheon-sejong-2025/scoreboard.webp)
_Final scoreboard. PTIT.BlueCyber is 10th with 3,764 points._

Only the top 7 got prizes. 7th place finished on 3,878, so we were 114 points short, less than a single challenge. Top 10 at an international final is a result I am happy with, but when you have seen your name in 4th and then miss a prize by less than one solve, that gap is the part that stays with you.

![The team at table 33 with our HackTheon medals](/assets/img/posts/hacktheon-sejong-2025/team-medals.webp)
_After the final, with the medals._

The awards were the next morning, July 11. After that the trip stopped being about the scoreboard. We went up to Seoul, met some Vietnamese friends living in Korea, and ended up out on the streets from night until morning, neon everywhere, walking and talking and laughing the contest off. We had flown over for a CTF and came home with a night none of us had planned.

![A night street in Seoul](/assets/img/posts/hacktheon-sejong-2025/seoul-night.webp)
_Seoul at night._

This was also one of the last contests for PTIT.BlueCyber as a team, so it meant a little more than a result. We went hoping to bring a prize back and did not, but I would take this trip again without a second thought.

Every finalist got the same black case, with the medal, stickers, a USB card, an NIS gift and the competition badge inside.

![The HackTheon Sejong 2025 finalist case with medal, badge and stickers](/assets/img/posts/hacktheon-sejong-2025/swag.webp)

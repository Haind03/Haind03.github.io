Every list has a first entry, and this is mine. In 2022 I played PTIT CTF, the Student Information Security Competition run by the Faculty of Information Security at PTIT, and finished with a consolation prize. It was the first time I won anything in a CTF, and the first time I stood on a stage holding a certificate with my name on it for something I'd done at a keyboard.

## Before

I'd joined ISP Club not long before, and at that point CTF was still mostly a thing other people were good at. I knew enough to get through the easy challenges and not much more. PTIT CTF is individual Jeopardy across web, crypto, pwn and reverse engineering, so there was nowhere to hide: no teammate to take the categories I was weak at, just me, the board, and a lot of challenges that looked impossible until suddenly they weren't.

![Playing the PTIT CTF 2022 final in the yellow ISP shirt](/assets/img/posts/ptit-ctf-2022/playing.webp)
_Somewhere in the middle of the PTIT CTF 2022 final, yellow ISP shirt and all._

## During

What I remember most about that final is how loud my own head was. Every challenge I couldn't crack felt like proof that I didn't belong there, and every flag that went through felt like a fluke. That's normal for a first real contest, and I know that now, but it didn't feel normal then. What kept me going was the part of CTF I still love most: the moment a binary or a broken piece of code finally makes sense and you see exactly what the author was hiding. I chased that feeling all day, mostly in the reversing challenges, which is probably the first hint of where I'd end up.

## After

When the results came out I had a consolation prize. It's the smallest prize the contest gives, and I couldn't have been happier with it. Going up on that stage with the other winners, in the club shirt, holding flowers and a framed certificate, is a memory I'll keep for a long time.

![PTIT CTF 2022 award winners on stage](/assets/img/posts/ptit-ctf-2022/awards-stage.webp)
_The PTIT CTF 2022 awards ceremony._

More than the prize, what I took away was a direction. A consolation prize meant I could compete with the people in that room, and it also showed me exactly how far I was from the top. I spent the next year closing that gap, and when I played the same contest again in 2023 I came home with [second prize](/posts/ctf-ptit-ctf-2023/). Everything else in my CTF list, the ASEAN finals, Korea, Russia, started on this stage.

## Thanks

Thank you to the Faculty of Information Security and PTIT for running a contest that gives students a real shot at a real stage, to the sponsors who supported it, and to ISP Club for pulling me in and teaching me nearly everything I knew at the time. If you're a student who's just joined and wondering whether it's worth entering when you feel like you know nothing: it is. I did, and this is where it started.
